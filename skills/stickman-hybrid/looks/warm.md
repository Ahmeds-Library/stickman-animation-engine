# warm

**When:** ancient humans, early civilisations, prehistory, night survival, religion and myth, Indian
history, and Urdu / Hindi explainer channels. This is the benchmark look of **Zenn** (8.1M views) and top-tier historical animation channels.

**STYLE** (paste this after `look: warm`):

warm hand-drawn 2D cinematic cartoon illustration for a historical explainer, thick clean dark-brown ink outlines (#2B1E16, 7-9px weight), flat warm colours with soft painterly shading and subtle paper grain, golden ochre and amber dominant palette for daylight (#EEEBE1 to #D4A373), high-contrast midnight indigo with glowing campfire amber for night/peril sequences (#181A2A with #FFAE33 firelight), a full-bleed atmospheric background that fills the entire frame (never a blank white screen), cinematic directional lighting, simple and highly readable, 16:9 or 9:16. People are simple round-head stick-figure characters with dot eyes, expressive brows, and thin limbs.

**Palette & Lighting Tokens:**
- Daylight Canvas: Warm Eggshell Parchment `#EEEBE1` / Golden Sand `#D4A373`
- Night & Peril Canvas: Midnight Indigo `#181A2A` / Deep Shadow Navy `#303D97`
- Fire & Safety Glow: Radiant Campfire Amber `#FFAE33` / Torch Flame `#FF7800`
- Ink Outlines: Deep Walnut Ink `#2B1E16` or Obsidian `#121212`

**Character sheet:** use the same prompt as `bright`, with two changes:
- the backdrop is "a warm golden-ochre textured paper backdrop with a soft vignette"
- the drawing style is "thick clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat warm colours with soft
  painterly shading"

**Notes:**
- A whole film in this look turns muddy brown-orange if not broken up. Follow **The Dual-Tone Canvas Harmony (Rule 50)**:
  - Day / Comfort scenes: Golden ochre and warm eggshell parchment.
  - Peril / Night / Wilderness scenes: Midnight indigo and shadow silhouettes illuminated solely by dynamic campfire or torchlight.
- Food or craft close-ups pick up white-headed background cooks unless the prompt says "no people".
